14.0 OUTSIDE REVIEWS AND THEIR RESOLUTION [C]

14.1 The reviews

Two outside reviews were made on 2 November 2026: the first of this specification at Revision B, the second of the companion Detailed Specification (DS). Both are kept as received in docs/reviews/. Their common judgement: the architecture is sound and worth keeping (geometry separate from drawing, declared handles, shared points as shared data, the makers–shapers–connectors model); the weaknesses are at the boundaries between features, where requirements conflicted or left behaviour undecided. Every finding was accepted. The resolutions are the contracts R-1 to R-19 of docs/RECONCILIATION.md, which govern over any earlier text of this specification or the DS that differs. The foundation prototype was built to prove the ones the reviews found most dangerous.

14.3 What the prototype proved

The foundation prototype (docs/CHECKLIST_foundation.md) is one page the owner can use: Parabolas with four handles, welds by dropping one handle on another, moves and rotations (refused when they would pull a shared or attached point), attachments half way along another layer's arm, nails, undo and redo, save and open with conflict detection, crash recovery, a true-scale SVG nail template, and the Settings page. Nineteen unit tests prove the contracts, seventeen page checks drive the page as the owner would, and a check enforces the layer rules. The page test found one real bug (a click counted as a move and discarded the redo history), which is the kind of finding documents cannot make.
